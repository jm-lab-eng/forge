01About

Engineering systems that perform in the field

I am a Mechanical Design Engineer with four-plus years at Genrobotics, designing hydraulic and electro-mechanical equipment for oil & gas, sanitation and medical applications, from concept and detailed design through prototyping, qualification and field deployment.

My work centres on mechanical and structural design, hydraulic system engineering and FEA in ANSYS, with SolidWorks and AutoCAD for 3D models and manufacturing drawings. I apply GD&T, tolerance analysis and DFM/DFA, and I work directly with fabrication teams and external suppliers to resolve build and manufacturability issues.

On DRACO, a robotic tank-cleaning system for hazardous areas, I was the project owner for mechanical engineering. That covered the hydraulic circuit, structural design and analysis, resolving structural deflection and joint movement, component selection, design review and technical guidance to the engineering team. I also supported the PESO approval documentation.

I hold a Lean Six Sigma Black Belt, which I use for structured root-cause analysis. I am based in the UAE, available to join immediately, and open to roles across the country, including Abu Dhabi and Dubai.
